# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
University students planning revision for several courses at once, usually around a semester or finals period. They open the app on a phone between lectures and on a laptop when planning. Their job: know what to study today, see whether the whole exam period fits their time, and recover when a session is missed.

## Product Purpose
StudySync turns a list of courses, exam dates, difficulty and available hours into a day-by-day revision timetable, then helps the student follow it: today's sessions, progress, rescheduling, and export to their calendar. Success is a student who opens it each day, ticks sessions off, and reaches each exam having covered the planned hours.

## Positioning
A revision planner that plans backwards from exam dates and weights hours by urgency and difficulty, with no account and no server: the plan lives in the browser and can be shared as a link or exported to a calendar.

## Operating Context
Used daily in short visits (check today, tick a session, reschedule a missed one) and occasionally for longer planning (create a plan, adjust, export to PDF or a calendar app). Phone and laptop. Data is stored in browser localStorage only.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Browser-only. No account, no backend, no API keys. Plans are stored in localStorage; sharing must work without a server (plan encoded in the link).
- The schedule is generated by a deterministic rule-based algorithm in `src/lib/timetableGenerator.ts` (urgency from days-to-exam and difficulty, rest days, daily hours, time-of-day preference). It is not AI and the interface must not claim it is.
- Stack: Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, zustand, react-router, date-fns, jsPDF, dnd-kit. Deploys to Vercel as a static site.
- Existing features to keep: multi-step plan creation, week calendar and list views, edit sessions, PDF export, calendar (.ics) export, share link, light and dark themes.
- Undecided: reminders and notifications (not reliable in a browser-only app), multi-device sync.

## Brand Commitments
Name: StudySync. A logo exists at `src/components/Logo.tsx`. No other brand rules were given.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, user counts or benchmarks exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Today first: the first screen answers what to study now.
- Honest tool: describe the scheduler as rule-based, never as AI; keep data on the device and say so.
- Recoverable: missed or wrongly planned sessions can be rescheduled in one step, and destructive actions can be undone.
- Works on a phone: every action is reachable by touch, not only by hover.
